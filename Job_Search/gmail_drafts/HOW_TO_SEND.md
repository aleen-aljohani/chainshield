# How to send the drafts (2 clicks each)

**40 company drafts** were created in the connected Gmail account (**fx.py2005@gmail.com**), each addressed
to that company's real contact email from your NCA-list file, personalized with the company name, using your
approved email text. Full list: `Job_Search/drafts_created.csv`.

## Why I could not send them for you
- This session's Gmail tool **creates drafts only — there is no send capability**, so no email was or can be sent from here.
- The tool also **cannot reliably attach your CV** (the file would have to be embedded, by hand, in every draft — which risks corrupting the PDF and does not scale to 40). So attaching is left as a one-click manual step.

## To send each one (in Gmail)
1. Open **Drafts** in Gmail (account: fx.py2005@gmail.com).
2. Open a draft (subject: "Cybersecurity Graduate / Entry-Level Application – Aleen Saleh Aljohani").
3. Click the **paperclip** and attach `Aleen_Aljohani_Cybersecurity_CV.pdf`.
4. Click **Send**.

Tip: keep the CV file open in a tab so you can attach quickly across all drafts.

## Important notes
- The drafts are sent from **fx.py2005@gmail.com**, not from your CV email (aleensaljohani@gmail.com).
  If you want replies to go to your CV address, either send from that account or add it as the Reply-To.
- These are `info@`/`contact@` general inboxes for most companies (the recruitment address `careers@mobily.com.sa`
  is the exception) — fine for a general application, but also apply on official career portals where available.
- 4 earlier drafts are role-based/self-addressed templates (SOC, GRC, IAM, Accenture) — optional extras.
